# AI-assisted development

Oripo was developed with Codex assistance. The owner selected the product direction, references and scope. Codex implemented folding algorithms, authored model data and draft manuscripts, and exercised the local test suite. Git records the maintainer's chosen commit identity. Each model's provenance record tracks its construction, rights and outstanding reviews.

For future lessons, the owner can give an external assistant a specific reference, the native schema, representative packages and the authoring guide. A draft can identify missing crease information or unsupported motions. Codex or a contributor reviews it against the source, corrects geometry, verifies rights, integrates the manuscripts and tests the production studio. Owner visual acceptance and human linguistic review are separate decisions. [Draft requirements and review workflow](authoring.md) · [Construction references](phase-9-candidates.md)

The geometry fixtures and browser projections include independent calculations by the implementer. They provide repeatable numerical and visual comparisons. Physical folding trials, independent maker ratings, real phones and spoken screen-reader review remain pending. [Local verification record](release.md)
