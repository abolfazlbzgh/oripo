The four-wing locked butterfly is rated intermediate by [Origami Diagrams](https://origamidiagrams.com/diagrams/butterfly/). Origami Guide attributes its similar easy-intermediate waterbomb butterfly to Akira Yoshizawa. Kosmulski also documents a distinct [traditional butterfly](https://www.origami.kosmulski.org/instructions/butterfly-traditional). Those attributions do not establish rights to the specific locked-body design. The [eight-petal flower](https://origami.guide/?p=10186) needs a layered center sink and eight everted petals. The [tulip head](https://origami.guide/intermediate-origami/origami-tulip-flower/) needs two retained tucks during inflation and uses a separate stem in the reference. The Masu gusset carries two triangles between an upright post and a moving wall. Its constraints provide neither the pocket inflation nor petal eversion. The map supplies a distinct geometric category and a checkable endpoint while these flower mechanisms remain open.

Physical-paper trials for both selections are **NOT RUN**. Publication depends on the geometry, material-layer and production gates.
