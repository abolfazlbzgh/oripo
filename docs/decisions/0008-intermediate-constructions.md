The map supplies a distinct geometric category and a checkable endpoint while these flower mechanisms remain open.

## Implemented construction

The frog opens with paired gates at x = ±0.5 on a 2 × 2 square. They produce the split upper ply of the 1 × 2 strip. OrigamiOK rates the square, two-gate, pulled-foot construction Intermediate. Origami Way also rates its four-foot pocket-and-pleat sequence Intermediate and starts by doubling the square lengthwise. Oripo authors its own crease coordinates, bilateral actions, text and 3D rest angle. These references establish the paper model and human difficulty. The project distributes its own meshes, manuscripts, tests and projected images under the repository license. [Square gatefold sequence](https://origamiok.com/how-to-make-an-easy-origami-frog/) · [Pocket and pleat sequence](https://www.origamiway.com/origami-jumping-frog/)

1. The upper square is centered at (0, 0.5) with radius 0.5. Two diagonals and a reverse transverse mark define its waterbomb. The lower triangle holds still while the upper triangle rotates through 180°. Fixed distances from the center and the two bordering corners determine the inward branch for each side midpoint. The guide follows six material segments through the collapse. The doubled stock lands in eight ordered plies.
2. The free upper corners rise and splay to (±0.3, 0.4). The tail doubles at y = −0.5, the waist narrows at x = ±0.25 under the forelegs, and the rear edge folds up at y = −0.25. Diagonal marks locate the two pocket mouths. Each has a stationary diagonal and three moving material groups, the lip, gusset and cover. Reflecting the held diagonal across the plane containing the moving rim and fixed edge gives the free tip. At half progress the rim stands √2/8 above the base and the tip stands √2/6 above it. Four probe points enclose a tetrahedron of volume √2/768. Across the flattened pocket a probe crosses eight distinct plies at the foot and sixteen at its root.
3. Each rear knee turns out to (±0.3, −0.4). The first spring crease doubles at y = 0. The next crease, one eighth of a unit away, returns 150°. Easing the first bend by 18° lifts the return hinge by 0.038627 units. The last instruction shows the unloaded folded shape. Elastic force, takeoff and flight remain physical tests.

The map has four half-unit transverse bands on a 3 × 2 rectangle. The four oblique marks have pitch 0.75 and shift laterally by 0.25 across one band. Each mark goes through the accordion and reopens. Releasing the three accordion creases leaves four zigzags. At each interior vertex the gather preserves the two edge lengths and their scalar product. At the authored endpoint cos θ = 3/5, projected row spacing is √5/6 and the alternate ridges stand 0.6 units above the troughs. The two straight side edges trim the outer columns of the underlying periodic pattern. The 20 panels have positive projected areas in plan view. This defines an open relief with a clear channel between the ridges. ## Version 3 authoring contract

An action has a stable `id`, positive `durationMs`, registered `guides`, empty `turns` and either a `field` covering the sheet or `regions` of unique face IDs. Unassigned faces in a regional operation stay still. Each affected face must already be subdivided along its material hinges. The starting sheet for a field lies in the XY plane and may contain folded packets. Ordinary signed turns remain available in surrounding steps, including after the model has become spatial.

| Field           | Parameters                                                   | Material constraint                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `waterbomb`     | center, radius, optional number of stacked plies             | Triangulate the square on its diagonal rays and side-midpoint rays. Keep the lower cap and attached extension fixed.         |
| `pocket-squash` | center, inward sign, sector, landing ply                     | Assign the lip, gusset and cover to named material packets around one pin. Mirroring changes the handedness of the movement. |
| `herringbone`   | grid origin, column pitch, band height, stagger, final angle | Split at every transverse and oblique crease. The final angle must leave positive row spacing.                               |

The compiler samples agreement across every shared vertex before storing the endpoint. Each face supplies a translated orthonormal pair of axes. Its cross product transports the existing drawing offset, and the material coordinates remain those of the welded sheet. The studio and static projection accept the same virtual ply thickness. The frog uses 0.0002 world units to distinguish closely packed plies, and the map uses the existing default of 0.0007.

Primitive fixtures use their own sphere-intersection, reflection and parallelogram constructions. The full-model tests separately compare every face diagonal, all incident transforms, fixed hinges, canonical landmarks, layer intersections of local probe rays, and transverse collisions at 31 interior fractions per action. Browser outline fixtures construct the frog boundary from the feet, head and Z-pleat landmarks and the map from its row and column spacing. [Numerical and browser results](../release.md)
