## Twin-stern junk

Cassell's _Book of In-door Amusements, Card Games, and Fireside Fun_, third edition, gives thirteen figures under “Paper Chinese Junk.” The separate 1873 _Popular Recreator_ plate numbers its illustrations 4–16. The full historical plates were inspected. Senbazuru's compact boat establishes the visual target and a Medium rating. Its video sequence was unavailable. An experimental continuation of the historical method reached the square packet and rolled rims corresponding to figure 8. Its box inversion and subsequent frame pull left the layer path to the two seats unresolved. The integrated Oripo finish uses a scored rectangular floor, shorter seat tongues and straighter sides. Its 117 vertices, 224 edges and 108 faces subdivide one square. The supplied ten-action graph had 29 vertices, 52 edges and 24 faces. The project authored the new geometry, text and renderings on the public-domain historical preparation. [Package and moving face selections](../../models/twin-stern-junk/lesson.json)

## Reed Runner dinosaur

Jo Nakashima's July 2015 Velociraptor and all 44 numbered panels of the five-page diagram were inspected. The designer rates it Intermediate. A convention class using the video lists High Intermediate with wet shaping. The reviewed material did not establish permission to distribute a translated interactive adaptation. The separate Totodu74 record licenses the illustrated Brontosaur diagram and leaves the underlying design rights unresolved. No creator was contacted. [Designer and diagram](https://jonakashima.com.br/2015/09/05/origami-velociraptor-diagrams-and-video/) · [Convention class](https://origamiusa.org/classes/convention/2025/velociraptor) · [Brontosaur file record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Diagramme_brontosaure.jpg)

Reed Runner is an original low-relief walking dinosaur. Its blunt wedge head, broader torso, short forelimb, splayed hind legs and laterally turned tail distinguish it from the shaped Velociraptor. The research batch supplied a generic four-panel reverse fixture and no dinosaur graph. The project authored the 119-vertex, 222-edge, 104-face square, all thirteen actions, the manuscripts and the renders. [Package and moving face selections](../../models/reed-runner-dinosaur/lesson.json)
