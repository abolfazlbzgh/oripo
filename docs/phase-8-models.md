## Phase 8 starting assessment

The compiler accepts disjoint packets rotating about axes fixed in each step's starting pose. A material seam between packets must lie on every incident hinge. The flat authoring helper splits selected plies and can select an earlier packet. Neither mechanism carries a shared off-axis corner through a collapse. Render-only layer offsets cannot supply the missing material motion.

## Alternatives considered

OrigamiUSA lists Michael G. LaFosse's Kawasaki tribute butterfly as Intermediate. That is a better-supported human tier for an insect lesson. It is a named contemporary design. Permission to publish an adaptation and an independent construction remain open. [OrigamiUSA class](https://www.origamiusa.org/online-class/world-origami-days/2026/day-2-tribute-fold-kawasaki-butterfly)

Brent Marshall rates his Full Suspension Mountain Bike Advanced. His plan calls for a bird-base frame, two separately folded waterbomb-base wheels, sinks and pocket assembly. It would need multi-sheet instruction and a reliable connection between the finished units. The Toronto society identifies the diagram copyright and requires consent for republication. It was ruled out for this single-sheet increment. [Designer’s plan](https://origamitoronto.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/diagrams/mountainbike1.pdf) · [Society’s terms](https://origamitoronto.org/diagrams/)

Color studies for the five unbuilt models are cobalt and cornflower for butterfly (#3659ad, #7b9ede), leaf and moss for frog (#57804a, #86a863), ivory and cool shadow for crane (#ede6d8, #bac4cf), mauve for lily (#d5b6dc, #a88aba), and pink for lotus (#d36b90, #e6a0b7). These are design notes. Only validated lessons supply runtime palettes.
